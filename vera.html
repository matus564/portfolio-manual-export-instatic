Context

Retro is an editorial experiment exploring what happens when nostalgic imagery meets a strict, contemporary visual system. The project plays with contrast — structured layouts against chaotic imagery, refined typography against raw textures. I used a rigid grid to keep everything under control while deliberately letting the imagery push against it. It was less about overthinking and more about seeing how far the system could go before things started falling apart.

Atelier Vera is a conceptual brand and web design project for a fictional Parisian modeling agency. Instead of a typical flashy fashion site, I wanted to slow things down and focus on quiet luxury, warm neutrals, and clean typography.

I kept the layout simple and stripped back so the blurry, high-movement photography could really breathe. It was a fun exercise in restraint — seeing how little you actually need on a screen to make a brand feel confident and timeless.
