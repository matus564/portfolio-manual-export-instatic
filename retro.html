Retro

Scope

Product design concept

Editorial print

This project is an experimental editorial concept designed as a high-end visual homage to raw retro energy. Built on a strict multi-column Swiss grid system, the layout balances expansive white space with dense, vivid archival imagery.

Spatial tension and high-contrast typography elevate nostalgic imagery into a refined, contemporary print publication. The resulting conceptual framework explores how minimalist structural alignment can bring order and clarity to expressive historical content.
