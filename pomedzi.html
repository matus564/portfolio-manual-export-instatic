About

This project is a conceptual visual identity for an educational NGO centered around experiential learning and connection to nature. Rejecting digital perfection, the logotype utilizes soft geometry and custom line flows to communicate hands-on education and natural biodiversity. A rustic foundation of warm cream and sage green is inspired by foliage and recycled textures, balanced by soft lavender accents for creative energy. The resulting visual system provides a flexible, organic framework designed to scale effortlessly across digital touchpoints and event graphics.
