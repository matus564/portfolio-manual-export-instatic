Make the difference

My focus is on building bold visual identities, clean websites, and scalable design systems that rely on clarity and systematic design rather than short-lived trends. I look at every project through a bigger scope: combining sharp creativity with business logic to build sustainable visual systems that don't just look impressive, but actually work for your brand long-term.

Services & capabilities

01. Brand Identity & Strategy

I build visual systems from the ground up or evolve existing brands to match their true market position. From core messaging and visual character to typography systems, color logic, and comprehensive brand guidelines—ensuring long-term consistency and instant recognition.

What's included: Strategy & Positioning, Logo System, Typography & Color Rules, Touchpoint Design, Full Brand Manual.‍

Timeframe: approx. 3 – 6 weeks.

Investment:starting at € 2,000.

02. Web Design & Digital Experience

From high-converting landing pages to bespoke digital experiences. Every website is designed and built with a clear structure, strong typography, and intentional user journeys that convert visitors into clients.

What's included:UI/UX Design, Wireframing, Custom Responsive Development, CMS Integration, Motion Details, SEO & Speed Optimization.

Timeframe:prox. 4 – 6 weeks.

Investment:starting at € 2,000.

My proccess

01. Discovery & Alignment

We start with a kick-off call to discuss your vision, business goals, target audience, and scope. We align on expectations, deliverables, budget, and project timelines.

02. Notion Client Portal Setup

Once we agree to move forward, I set up your custom Notion Client Hub. This becomes our central command center for all communication, feedback loops, project milestones, and assets—ensuring a seamless, transparent workflow.

03. Strategy, Positioning & Moodboards

We explore your brand’s core purpose and visual positioning. I present strategic directions and visual moodboards to establish the overall look, feel, and narrative before jumping into design.

04. Concept Development & Direction

I design 2 distinct visual directions for your brand or website. We review both concepts together, and you select the direction that best fits your vision. To keep the process efficient, 2 rounds of refinements are included to polish the chosen direction to perfection.

05. Brand Identity & Touchpoint Design

With the approved direction, I expand the core concept into a complete visual identity system and apply it across real-world touchpoints. To ensure every detail is refined and perfectly aligned, this phase includes 2 rounds of refinements.

06. Full Brand Manual

Once the visual identity is fully approved, I document the entire system into a detailed, interactive Brand Manual. This serves as a single source of truth for your brand, covering typography rules, color logic, grid systems, logo usage, and design guidelines.

07. Implementation

All production-ready design files, digital assets, and guidelines are organized and handed over directly through your Notion Hub. If applicable, we proceed directly into custom web development, digital implementation, or print production.
