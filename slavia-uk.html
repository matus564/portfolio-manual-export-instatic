About

This project covers the complete design and Webflow development of a modern club management and presentation platform for a canoe club. Strong, athletic typography paired with a vibrant aquatic blue palette captures the energy of high-tempo racing and open-water training. Fluid, wave-like dividers break the rigid grid system to symbolize dynamic movement while maintaining clear information hierarchy for members. Through strategic asset optimization and clean site architecture, page load times were slashed from 3.3s down to 1.4s.
