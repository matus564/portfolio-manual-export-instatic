Slavia UK

Scope

UX/UI design

Web development

What client does

Sprint canoe club

Live site (design may differ)

Live site

Context

Slavia UK needed a new website that could work for two very different audiences: competitive athletes and parents trying to figure out what is happening at the club. The goal was to make the site feel as energetic as the sport itself while keeping the friendly atmosphere. I redesigned and developed the website with stronger typography, brighter visuals and a structure built around the way the club actually works. The result was a faster, clearer experience, with load time dropping from 3.3 seconds to 1.4.
