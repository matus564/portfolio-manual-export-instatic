About

Swiss Dental Clinic is a high-end visual identity system designed around Swiss precision, clinical trust, and modern minimalism.
The logo pairs raw typographic simplicity with a subtle cross icon, eliminating unnecessary decorative elements in favor of stark geometry.
A pristine white and gray palette creates a hygienic foundation, while targeted red accents direct visual hierarchy across all touchpoints.
The result is an authoritative, timeless brand framework that seamlessly scales across physical signage, digital media, and clinical collateral.
