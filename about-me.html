My view

Bullshit era

We live in an era where almost everything is driven by marketing and the pressure for speed. Everything moves faster, everythink gets produced cheaper, and somehow still needs to look exactly like everything else. AI has made making things easy. Making something worth looking at is still the tricky part.

That's probably why I care so much about work that feels considered. Something with an idea behind it. Something that doesn't look like it was made just to tick a box and move on.

I believe in

Beyond aesthetics

Pretty is not enough.
A logo can look great and still do absolutely nothing. Good design should give a brand something useful: a clear identity, a recognisable presence, and a reason for people to remember it.

Minimalism & clarity

Every typeface, colour, image and layout choice should have a reason to be there. If removing something makes the idea clearer, I remove it. Minimalism isn't about making everything empty and white. It's about getting rid of the noise until the important stuff has room to breathe.

Human chemistry

Design is collaborative. Which means communication matters. A lot. I like working with people who are honest, curious, respectful and comfortable saying "I don't know" when they don't know. No unnecessary drama. No 47-slide presentations explaining why a circle represents unity. Just a clear process, honest conversations and two people trying to make something good.

Zero apologies

You know that moment when someone asks for your website and you suddenly become very interested in changing the subject? I'd rather avoid that. My goal is to give you something you're genuinely proud to put in front of people.
Something you can send without explaining.
Show without apologising.
Look at without thinking.

“Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.”

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
