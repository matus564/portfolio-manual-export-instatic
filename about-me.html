My view

Bullshit era

We live in an era where almost everything is driven by marketing and the pressure for speed. The world is flooded with generic AI designs. Too many things today are done just to "get it over with"—do it as quickly as possible, take the money, and move on. The result is a massive amount of low-quality, superficial work that lacks any real foundation. That is why this monotony drains me. And it’s exactly why I deeply appreciate design that manages to truly step out of the crowd.

Values

Beyond aesthetics

It is not about creating a pretty logo and a website. It is about creating visual tools that help brands stand out, connect with real people, and command the premium value they deserve.

Minimalism & clarity

Good design is not about adding more; it’s about stripping away the noise until only the pure essence remains. I cut through the clutter to deliver visuals that speak clearly and instantly.

Human chemistry

At the end of the day, design is a collaborative craft that only works when there is mutual respect, clear communication, and a pleasant, human relationship between us. I believe that a calm, transparent process and a good connection are just as important as the final product—because great work can only happen when both sides actually enjoy working together.

Zero apologies

I don’t offer you a logo and a website. I offer you confidence. The freedom to hand over your business card or share your link without hesitation or apologies. That is the priceless feeling of absolute pride when presenting your brand to clients, partners, and competitors alike.

“Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.”

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
